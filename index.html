Questions

How does the free trial work?

You get 30 days of the full plan. If you don't cancel before day 30, your plan renews monthly or yearly, whichever you picked.

Do my clients need to pay or make an account?

No. Clients join free from your invite link and open the app on their phone.

What is the Solo plan?

Solo is for people training on their own: logging, PB wall, measurements, habits, progress photos and suggested progression. You can link to a coach later.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes, any time from your account. Upgrades take effect straight away.

Where are progress photos stored?

On the client's own phone. They can choose to share a photo with their coach.

What payment methods do you take?

Card payments through Stripe. Prices are in Philippine pesos. Manage or cancel any time from your account.
